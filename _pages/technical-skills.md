### Simulations developed

- Cross-bispectrum estimator for all unique triangles (used in [arXiv:2604.15440](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.15440))
- GPU-accelerated multi-line intensity mapping simulations (21 cm, CII, CO)
- SBI pipeline for multi-line intensity mapping and their cross-correlations
